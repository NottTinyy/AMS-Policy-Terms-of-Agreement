AMS | THE PADDOCK

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 August 2026

This policy explains what information the AMS | The Paddock Discord application ("the Bot") processes, why it is processed, and how members can make privacy requests.

1. Who we are

The Bot is operated for Ashesi Motorsport Society ("AMS", "The Paddock", "we", "us", or "our") to administer its motorsport community and competitions on Discord.

2. Information the Bot may process

Depending on enabled features, the Bot may process limited Discord-related information such as:

  • Discord user ID and display name/username;
  • server membership and relevant role information needed for administration;
  • driver/team registration information supplied for competitions;
  • race-session check-in and attendance responses;
  • competition results, standings, Penalty Points, stewarding outcomes and related administrative records;
  • information voluntarily submitted through official reports, appeals or Bot commands; and
  • technical information needed to operate the Bot, such as command interactions and error logs.

3. Information we do not intentionally request

The Bot is not intended to collect passwords, Discord authentication tokens, payment-card information, government identification numbers, or unrelated sensitive personal information. Members should not submit such information through Bot commands, race reports or public channels.

4. How we use information

  • identify members and drivers in competition records;
  • process session check-ins and attendance;
  • maintain team rosters and championship standings;
  • administer Penalty Points and sporting sanctions;
  • review incident reports and appeals;
  • communicate race-session information and notices;
  • protect the integrity and fairness of competitions; and
  • diagnose technical problems with the Bot.

5. Discord and third-party processing

The Bot operates through Discord. Discord independently processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. The Bot may receive information from Discord that is necessary for requested functionality.

6. Public competition information

Some competition information may intentionally be visible to members of The Paddock, including driver names/display names, team rosters, results, standings, attendance status and sporting decisions. Incident reports and appeals may also be archived by Stewards for transparency and future reference under the Paddock rules.

7. Data retention

Competition and administrative records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the season, resolve disputes, maintain accurate standings, enforce sporting decisions, preserve historical competition records, or meet legitimate administrative requirements. Records no longer reasonably required should be deleted or anonymized where practical.

8. Security

AMS takes reasonable measures to limit access to Bot records to authorized Executive Committee members, Stewards or other designated officials who need that access for administration. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your privacy requests

Members may contact the AMS/The Paddock Executive Committee through the official Discord server to ask what personal information relating to them is held by the Bot, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion where compatible with competition, disciplinary and administrative recordkeeping needs.

10. Children's privacy

The Bot is intended for members permitted to use Discord and participate in the relevant club activities. We do not intentionally request personal information from children.

11. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The latest version should be made available through official AMS/The Paddock resources.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the AMS/The Paddock Executive Committee through the official Discord server.

13. Third-party notice

Discord is a separate service. This policy does not replace Discord's Privacy Policy or Terms of Service. Members should review Discord's current policies for information about Discord's own processing.
