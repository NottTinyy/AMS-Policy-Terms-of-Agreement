1. Purpose

The Paddock is a motorsport community and competition operated by Ashesi Motorsport Society. The Bot supports race administration, including session announcements, driver check-ins, attendance records, open-lobby coordination, standings and other competition-management functions.

2. Participation

By using the Bot or participating in a Paddock activity, you agree to these Terms, the current Paddock Rules & Regulations, and applicable decisions of the Executive Committee and Stewards.

The Paddock runs F1 25 and Assetto Corsa Competizione competitions. Competition participation may require registration, an approved team or driver roster, and compliance with session-specific requirements.

3. Competition rules

The official Paddock rulebook governs sporting conduct. Cheating, exploits, unauthorized software, manipulated inputs and account sharing are prohibited. Drivers must race fairly, follow lobby instructions, respect officials, and comply with reporting and appeal procedures.

Attendance and check-in requirements apply to official sessions. Failure to check in, or confirming attendance and then failing to appear without prior notice, may result in Penalty Points under the current rulebook.

Steward decisions, appeals, Penalty Points, qualifying bans, race bans and other sporting consequences are administered under the current rulebook. The Executive Committee and appointed Stewards retain the authority specified by those rules.

4. Governance and disputes

The Paddock operates under an FIA-style governance model. The Executive Committee acts as the governing authority for Paddock championships. Contentious or high-profile incidents are reviewed by a panel of at least two Stewards, and a Steward with personal involvement must recuse themselves.

Appeals must follow the deadlines and evidence requirements in the current rulebook. Participation does not create a right to a particular race seat, team position, championship result, or penalty outcome.

5. Bot use

The Bot may create and update race-management messages, collect check-in responses, record attendance, display session information, and provide competition-related information. Bot features may change as AMS develops The Paddock.

The Bot is not a substitute for the official rulebook or an official ruling. Where a Bot display conflicts with an official ruling or the latest published rulebook, the official ruling and latest rulebook control.

6. User conduct

Users must not misuse the Bot, interfere with its operation, impersonate officials, manipulate competition records, submit knowingly false reports, or use the Bot to harass or threaten members. Communication in official report channels must remain respectful.

The Bot and related services are provided on a best-effort basis. AMS does not guarantee uninterrupted availability. Discord outages, game-server issues, technical failures or other circumstances may require a session to be rescheduled or administered manually.
